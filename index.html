Coordinate physics

Each position is a discrete triplet (x, y, z) with values 0–7. A 4-bit cell code stores colour and piece type. The full board needs 2,048 bits for occupancy.

White ascends +Z from Y=4, Z=1–2. Black descends −Z from Y=5, Z=7–8. The black rank mirrors X, so the kings face one another diagonally across X, Y and Z, with pawn lines close enough to engage.

Kings may not enter threatened cells. Check, checkmate and stalemate are evaluated in 3D.

Pawns promote to queens. No castling or en passant in this variant.
